Executive Summary

Work is progressing on all major fronts. The fuel gas reroute has a preferred routing behind the trailers and is out for proposal with Gallup. Heater shells for the new hot oil heaters (Item H-49200) have been received on site; the field bolting procedure is still being sourced, and WIC inspects the heaters and their concrete foundations Monday 8/10. Pump grouting checks by M4 came back clean, with final alignment and tie-in sign-off pending. A significant amount of fabrication is staged for hydro-testing, waiting on Azota's schedule. Kodiak unit commissioning prep has ten open items, mostly protection, responsibility assignments, and pre-burn-in verification.

1 · Fuel Gas Reroute

Awaiting Proposal
  • A more efficient reroute for the fuel gas line has been identified, running behind the trailers to minimize interference with equipment and traffic.
  • General arrangement drawings were sent to Gallup for a proposal on the scope of work.

2 · Hot Oil Heaters (H-49200)

Procedure Outstanding
  • Heater shells received on site.
  • The team is researching the correct procedure for bolting the shells together. No procedure has been found in Procore documents to date.
  • WIC is scheduled on-site Monday 2026-08-10 to inspect the heaters and their concrete foundations for excessive cracking.
Coordinator note: The GA drawing set (OPF dwg J251307, Item H-49200, Rev 1) contains no bolt-up torques or sequence — only hardware callouts (field-installed tube supports C4/C5/C9 at 34 places, 1" A193 B7 bolts; C9 shims added in Rev 1). The set on hand is marked superseded. Request the current revision and the erection/installation manual from OPF, referencing P.O. 24250-133, before shell bolt-up.

3 · Vessel Internal Inspections

Awaiting WIC
  • A list of all manways at the Titan plant was sent to WIC to determine which require opening for inspection, to avoid unnecessary work.
  • Access method for the contactor and amine still column inspections is pending decision: man-lift vs. scaffolding. Man-lift is the preferred option to save time; confirming with Gallup.

4 · Pump Alignment & Equipment Levelness

On Track
  • M4 completed the initial check on all pumps post-grouting — everything looking good.
  • M4 will return for final alignment and inspection before providing sign-off for tie-in.
  • A machinist level will be used to check the stabilizer Odell units for levelness (east/west and north/south).

5 · Hydro-Testing

Blocked on Schedule
  • A significant amount of fabrication work is ready for hydro-testing.
  • Clarification is needed from Azota on their plan and schedule (day or night shift) before testing can proceed.

6 · Kodiak Units — Commissioning Prep

Open Items

Task review on 8/8 identified the following preparation and protection items ahead of commissioning:

  • Protection: suction inlets need covering to keep debris out; verify whether the skid-mounted panels need shade covers to prevent overheating.
  • Control panel stairs: determine whether installation requires welding or can use existing brackets, and who performs the welding if required.
  • Fluids: confirm who is responsible for supplying and adding oil and coolant to the units.
  • Interconnect piping: status unknown — determine whether additional parts are expected.
  • Burn-in: check whether catalysts are still in the exhaust systems; they may need to be removed for the 24-hour burn-in.
  • Material control: label the blue aerial boxes and store them in the conics box; secure shim packages and filters so they don't walk away.
