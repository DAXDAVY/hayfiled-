The complete 40-sheet redlined P&ID set for the Hayfield Ridge Amine Facility was reviewed sheet-by-sheet at high magnification. Every red mark, cloud, strikethrough, and pink/magenta highlight was transcribed and converted to a punch item. One sheet is clean (HAY-PID-01002 Gas Cooler — printed “FUTURE SCOPE”, no marks); the other 39 sheets carry field markups.

Highest-risk findings (act first):
  • Missing relief devices. PSVs / relief assemblies are highlighted “Missing” at: V-22001 gas scrubber (1435 psig service), S-1100 inlet coalescing filter (PSV-1100, 1440 psig), E-2 reboiler thermal PSV, AC-1 amine cooler (PSV AC-1), all four VCU knock-out drums (PSV-301/-401/-302/-402), air system PSV-7002, generator fuel PSV-7006, plus missing PSV tailpipes at T-1 and F-71001. No pressure test / startup until resolved.
  • Overpressure routing question (HP pumps). Field notes PSV P-3C (set 1440 psig) relieves via 3"-V-0073-A2 to a 165# MAWP vessel — engineering must verify relief path adequacy.
  • Check valve installed backwards on 2"-DR-7001-F2AS (Blowcase System).
  • Instrument air cross-connected to the methanol inlet port on the JT Fuel Skid (connection E) — IA must be relocated and methanol connected.
  • Hot Oil system gaps not on ISOs: FCV-45001A control-valve station and PCV-2001 blanket-gas regulator “don’t exist / not on ISO’s”; burner vent valves XV-20003A / XV-20006A missing.
  • Safety instrumentation missing: level switch stacks (LSHH/LSH/LSL/LSLL) on all four KO drums; ESD/SDV wiring and tubing incomplete across the plant; slop-tank grounding station missing.

Schedule impact. The dominant themes are (1) relief-system completion, (2) instrument installation/wiring/tubing (largest item count — I&E), (3) small-bore valve additions (~60 valves ½"–3"), and (4) pipe supports/shoes/U-bolts. Long-lead risks: PSVs and the 14" valves (2 off) on the VCU vent headers, FCV-45001A (8" control valve), static mixer X-2, and orifice plates with specified bores. Several “SIZE HOLD” PSV vents remain unreleased by engineering — these block procurement. Underground (U/G) tie-ins noted on multiple headers should be completed before paving/backfill close-out.
